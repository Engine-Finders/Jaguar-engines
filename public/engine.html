Ingenium Family - 2.0 Diesel & Petrol

Jaguar's modern modular engine range, introduced in 2015. Available in diesel (AJ200D/204DTD) and petrol (AJ200P/204PT) variants, with later revisions carrying new codes.

204DTD / AJ200D
2.0 diesel - Ingenium D. Fitted to XE, XF, F‑Pace, E‑Pace. Known for timing chain tensioner issues (pre‑2019). 590 requests in 2025 [JAG‑VERIFIED]. 204PT / AJ200P
2.0 petrol - Ingenium P. More reliable than diesel, but cooling system issues reported. 500 requests in 2025 [JAG‑VERIFIED]. AJ20D6
Later 2.0 diesel Ingenium (post‑2019) with revised timing chain design. Improved reliability. AJ20P3
2.0 petrol Ingenium (P300) - higher output version, fitted to performance variants. AJ20P6
2.0 petrol Ingenium (P250) - mid‑power version, common in XE and F‑Pace.

Learn more about Ingenium reliability in our 204DTD failure rate and 204PT failure rate pages.

AJ‑V6 Family - 3.0 Supercharged & Earlier V6

Jaguar's V6 engines, spanning the older AJ‑V6 (used in S‑Type, X‑Type) and the modern 3.0 supercharged AJ126 (used in XF, XJ, F‑Type, F‑Pace).

AJ‑V6
3.0 naturally aspirated V6, used in S‑Type and early X‑Type. Simpler but less powerful. AJ126 3.0 V6 SC
3.0 supercharged V6. Used in XF, XJ, F‑Type, F‑Pace. Known for cooling system failures. 560 requests in 2025 [JAG‑VERIFIED]. AJ6 / AJ16
Classic straight‑six from the 1980s‑90s. Used in XJ‑40, XJS. Reliable, but now rare.

AJ‑V8 Family - 4.0, 4.2, 5.0 & 5.0 Supercharged

Jaguar's iconic V8 engines, from the early 4.0 and 4.2 to the 5.0 naturally aspirated and supercharged (AJ133). Fitted to XF, XJ, XK, F‑Type, and F‑Pace SVR.

AJ‑V8 (early)
4.0 and 4.2 V8s, used in XK, XJ, S‑Type. Known for timing chain tensioner wear on earlier units. AJ133 5.0 V8
5.0 naturally aspirated and supercharged (V8 SC). Pre‑2013 chain tensioner risk; post‑2013 much improved. 330 requests in 2025 [JAG‑VERIFIED].

Classic Jaguar Engines - XK6 & V12

The engines that defined Jaguar's heritage: the legendary XK6 straight‑six and the V12 that powered the XJS and early XJ models.

XK6
The iconic 2.4–4.2 litre straight‑six, used from the 1940s to the 1980s. Robust and characterful. V12
5.3 and 6.0 V12, used in XJS and XJ. A legend, but complex and expensive to maintain.

Ford‑Sourced Engines - Duratorq & Others

During the Ford ownership era, Jaguar used several Ford‑derived engines, primarily diesels, in the X‑Type and early XF.

224DT 2.2 Duratorq
2.2 diesel, used in X‑Type and early XF. Also fitted to Land Rover Freelander 2. Duratorq 2.0
2.0 diesel used in some X‑Type variants. Less common than the 2.2. Duratorq 2.2 (later)
Later 2.2 diesel, used in XF 2.2D. Reliable but DPF‑sensitive. GTDi 2.0
2.0 petrol turbo (EcoBoost‑based), used in some XF models. Rare in Jaguar.

Frequently Asked Questions - Jaguar Engines

How many Jaguar engine families are there?

We cover 17 distinct engine families on this site, spanning from the 1940s XK6 to the latest Ingenium 2.0. Each has its own Knowledge Centre with full details.

Which Jaguar engine is the most reliable?

Based on our data, the post‑2013 AJ133 V8 and the later Ingenium petrol (204PT, AJ20P6) show the fewest reported failures. However, reliability depends heavily on maintenance history. See our failure rate by engine for the full picture.

How do I identify which engine my Jaguar has?

Check your VIN or the engine code stamped on the block. Our engine code guide and engine number location pages will help you confirm.

Where can I find replacement costs for my Jaguar engine?

Each engine's Knowledge Centre includes a cost table for used, reconditioned, and rebuilt units, plus labour hours. You can also visit our economics centre for per‑model verdicts.
