How We Compile Our Case Studies

Every case study is an aggregate analysis of real‑world data — never a single owner story. We triangulate three proprietary channels.

  • 1. Enquiry descriptions — 24,650 free‑text problem statements from quote requests, categorised by engine and failure type.
  • 2. Supplier quote notes — mechanics’ actual diagnoses and repair recommendations, used to validate owner‑reported symptoms.
  • 3. Post‑sale surveys — 100+ owners surveyed 3‑6 months after replacement, capturing real costs and satisfaction.
  • 4. External validation — TSBs, forums, and market data (Auto Trader, Honest John) provide context, but our proprietary data is the backbone.

We never fabricate individuals or anecdotes. Every number is tagged ([EM‑VERIFIED], [EM‑QUOTE], [EM‑SURVEY], etc.) and traceable. For full detail, see our methodology page.
