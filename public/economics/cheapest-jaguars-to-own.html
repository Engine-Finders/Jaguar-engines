Cheapest Jaguars to Own The Ultimate Cost Guide

We've ranked every modern Jaguar model by total ownership cost – from purchase price and fuel to insurance, servicing, and the all-important engine repair bills. Real 2025 UK data reveals which Jaguar is kindest to your wallet.

The short answer: the Jaguar XE with the 2.0-litre Ingenium diesel (AJ200D) is the cheapest Jaguar to own, thanks to low purchase prices, decent fuel economy (55+ mpg), affordable servicing, and relatively modest engine repair costs. The XF follows closely, while the F-Type and XJ are the most expensive to run.

  • The XE's reconditioned engine supply-and-fit is £4,800–£7,000 [EM-QUOTE] – much lower than the AJ133 V8 at £8,000–£12,000
  • Insurance groups for the XE (typically 30–35) are lower than the F-Type (45+) [THIRD-PARTY]
  • Fuel economy: XE diesel 55–60 mpg vs F-Type V8 25–30 mpg [THIRD-PARTY]
  • Depreciation: the XE and XF hold value better than older XJs and XKs, but all Jaguars depreciate; the X-Type is the cheapest to buy but has high repair-to-value risk

Key Factors That Determine Ownership Cost

We've considered five main cost areas to build this ranking. Here's how each model performs.

1

Purchase Price & Depreciation

The XE and XF are the most affordable to buy used, and they hold value reasonably well. The X-Type is very cheap but depreciates quickly. The F-Type and I-Pace are expensive to buy, with steep initial depreciation.

2

Fuel Economy

Diesels (XE, XF, E-Pace, F-Pace) offer 40–60 mpg, saving hundreds per year. V8 petrol models (XK, F-Type R, XJ Supersport) are thirstiest, costing £2,000+ more annually in fuel.

3

Insurance & Servicing

Insurance groups range from 25 (X-Type) to 50 (F-Type SVR). Servicing costs are similar across modern Ingenium models, but older V8s require more frequent and expensive maintenance.

4

Engine Repair Costs

The Ingenium diesel (AJ200D) is the cheapest to replace (£4,800–£7,000 [EM-QUOTE]). The AJ133 V8 can cost over £10,000, making a failure on a low-value car a write-off.

5

The 60% Rule

Our repair-to-value threshold shows that older XJs and XKs with high repair costs often exceed 60% of the car's value, making them risky budget buys. See our framework page for details.

The Verdict: Which Jaguar Is Truly Cheapest to Own?

If you want the lowest total cost of ownership, the Jaguar XE 2.0d (AJ200D) is the clear winner. It offers a great blend of low purchase price, excellent fuel economy, and relatively affordable maintenance and repair costs.

🏆

Best Overall: XE 2.0d

Low entry price, 55+ mpg, insurance group 30–35, and engine replacement costs under £7,000. The XE is the smartest financial choice for most buyers.

🥈

Runner-Up: XF 2.0d

Similar running costs to the XE, with a bit more space and comfort. Slightly higher purchase price but still excellent value.

⚠️

Best Avoided for Budget: X-Type and S-Type

Though cheap to buy, their age means high repair-to-value ratios. A major engine failure can easily exceed the car's worth – see our scrap guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest Jaguar to run in the UK?

The Jaguar XE 2.0d (AJ200D Ingenium) is the cheapest to run, offering low purchase price, excellent fuel economy (55+ mpg), and relatively low repair costs. The XF 2.0d is a close second.

Is the Jaguar X-Type cheap to own?

The X-Type is cheap to buy (often under £3,000), but its age means high repair-to-value risk. A major engine failure can cost more than the car is worth – often a scrap case. We recommend checking our X-Type engine replacement guide.

Are diesel Jaguars cheaper to own than petrol?

Generally yes, especially for high-mileage drivers. Diesels offer significantly better fuel economy and similar servicing costs. However, diesel Ingenium engines have known timing chain issues (pre-2019) that can be costly; factor in the repair risk.

How much does it cost to replace a Jaguar engine?

Costs vary widely. For an AJ200D Ingenium diesel, supply-and-fit is £4,800–£7,000 [EM-QUOTE]. For an AJ133 V8, it's £8,000–£12,000 [EM-QUOTE]. See our Most Expensive Jaguar Repairs page for a full breakdown.

Is the I-Pace cheap to own?

The I-Pace has low running costs (electricity vs petrol) and low maintenance (no engine oil, filters, etc.). However, its high purchase price and potential battery replacement cost make it expensive overall. Depreciation is also steep.
