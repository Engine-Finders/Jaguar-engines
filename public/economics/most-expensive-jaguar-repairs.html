Most Expensive Jaguar Repairs Costs & Verdicts

The AJ133 V8 and AJ126 V6 top the list, with full reconditioned replacements hitting £9,000+ – often exceeding the value of older XFs and XJs. We rank the costliest repairs and show you exactly when to scrap.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most expensive Jaguar engine to replace?

The AJ133 5.0 V8 supercharged is the most expensive, with a reconditioned supply-and-fit costing £8,000–£12,000 [EM-QUOTE]. Its high parts cost and 14–18 hours of labour make it the costliest Jaguar repair.

Is an AJ126 failure always a write‑off?

No – it depends on the car. On an F‑Type or late F‑Pace, the repair is often worthwhile. But on an older XF or XJ with low value, the 60% rule often flags it as a scrap case. Use our calculator to check.

Are used engines cheaper for these repairs?

Yes – used supply costs are typically 40–60% lower than reconditioned. For the AJ133, a used unit is £3,000–£5,500 [EM-QUOTE] vs £5,500–£9,000 recon. However, used engines carry more risk; many owners prefer recon for the warranty.

Does the 60% rule apply to every Jaguar model?

The rule is constant, but the outcome varies. High‑value F‑Types and late F‑Paces clear the threshold easily; older XFs and XJs often fail it. Always check your car’s current value – not what you paid for it.
