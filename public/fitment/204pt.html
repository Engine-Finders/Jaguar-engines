The Short Answer

The Jaguar AJ200P / 204PT is a 2.0-litre turbocharged petrol Ingenium engine fitted to a range of Jaguar models from 2015 onwards. It powers the XE, XF, F-Pace, and E-Pace, among others, across various power outputs. To confirm if your Jaguar has this engine, check the engine code on the timing cover (visible when you open the bonnet) or use the Jaguar VIN decoder.

  • Power outputs: 200 PS (P200), 250 PS (P250), and 300 PS (P300) variants.
  • Key models: XE (X760), XF (X260), F-Pace (X761), E-Pace (X540).

How to Confirm Which Engine Is in Your Jaguar

There are three reliable ways to confirm whether your Jaguar is fitted with the AJ200P / 204PT engine. Use the method that best suits your situation.

  1. Check the engine code label.

    Open the bonnet and locate the engine code label on the timing cover or cylinder head. The code will be clearly stamped as 204PT or AJ200P. This is the most direct method.

  2. Use the Jaguar VIN decoder.

    Your vehicle's VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) can be decoded to reveal the engine code. The 8th character of the VIN indicates the engine type. For the 204PT, the 8th character will be one of the following: D (for 200 PS), E (for 250 PS), or F (for 300 PS). You can verify this using a free online Jaguar VIN decoder.

  3. Check your Jaguar's registration document.

    The V5C logbook includes the engine number, which can be cross-referenced with Jaguar's engine list. While this is less direct than the other methods, it is a useful secondary check if the engine label is not accessible.

Why Correct Engine Identification Matters

Getting the engine code right is critical — especially if you are considering a replacement. Installing the wrong engine variant can lead to:

  • ECU compatibility issues — The engine control unit is programmed for a specific power output and emissions setup.
  • Mounting and fitment problems — Even within the same family, slight variations in auxiliary components can prevent a direct fit.
  • Legal compliance failures — Installing an engine not originally homologated for your chassis can create MOT or insurance complications.
  • Voided warranty or specialist support — Most vetted Jaguar specialists will not work on mismatched engine/chassis combinations.

Always check the Engine Code Guide and consult a specialist before purchasing a used or reconditioned engine. The AJ200P is generally reliable, but cross-referencing the exact engine code with your vehicle's chassis ensures a smooth replacement.

Common Misidentifications: What the AJ200P Is Not

The AJ200P / 204PT is frequently confused with other Jaguar engines. Here’s what to look out for to avoid a costly mistake.

AJD / 204DTD Diesel Ingenium

The diesel Ingenium (204DTD) shares the same 2.0-litre displacement and basic architecture but is a completely different engine with a different fuel system, block design, and ECU mapping. It is not interchangeable with the petrol variant. Check the fuel filler cap and VIN to confirm.

AJ126 3.0 V6 Supercharged

The 3.0-litre V6 supercharged petrol (AJ126) is a completely different family of engine. While it appears in some of the same models (e.g., XE, XF), it is physically larger and requires different mounting points and ancillaries. Do not confuse it with the four-cylinder Ingenium.

AJ133 5.0 V8 Supercharged

The V8 is an entirely different proposition. While F-Pace SVR and XJ models use it, it is a performance-oriented, high-displacement engine with no relationship to the 2.0-litre Ingenium beyond the Jaguar badge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between AJ200P and 204PT?

AJ200P is Jaguar’s model designation for the 2.0-litre Ingenium petrol engine. 204PT is the internal engine code used by Jaguar Land Rover. They refer to the same engine. The 204PT code is found on the engine block itself.

How can I tell if my Jaguar has the 204PT engine without opening the bonnet?

The easiest way is to check your VIN. The 8th character of the VIN indicates the engine type. For the 204PT, it will be D (200 PS), E (250 PS), or F (300 PS). You can also use the Jaguar VIN decoder on this site.

Can I fit a 204PT engine from an XE into an F-Pace?

Yes, the engine block itself is interchangeable across Jaguar models, but you must check the ECU compatibility and ensure ancillary components (turbo, injectors, wiring loom) match the chassis. It is not a simple plug-and-play swap for most DIYers. Always consult a specialist.

Is the 204PT petrol Ingenium a reliable engine?

Generally, yes. The petrol Ingenium is considered more reliable than its diesel counterpart. However, common issues include water pump failures and occasional timing chain stretch, especially in earlier models. For a detailed verdict, see the AJ20P6 Knowledge Centre.
