F-Pace Engine Replacement: 80 Real UK Quotes Compared

Real 2025 UK enquiry and post-sale survey data on what F-Pace owners were quoted for engine replacement, what they actually paid, and whether they'd choose the same route again.

Of 80 Jaguar F-Pace owners seeking engine replacement quotes in our 2025 data, the average quoted price was £5,200–£7,800, with actual spend averaging 12% below the initial quote — and 86% of those who went ahead reported satisfaction with their decision three to six months later.

  • Sample: 80 Jaguar F-Pace (X761) enquiries with confirmed engine failure across diesel (AJ200D) and petrol (AJ200P) variants, 2025 [EM-VERIFIED]

  • Actual spend after negotiation/selection: 12% below initial quote on average [EM-SURVEY]
  • 86% of post-sale survey respondents reported satisfaction with their replacement choice 3–6 months later [EM-SURVEY]
  • Scrap rate: 8% of owners chose to scrap rather than replace, concentrated on vehicles with values under £10,000

The Data

Sample: 80 Jaguar F-Pace (X761) customers who requested an engine replacement quote through our network in 2025, covering both diesel (AJ200D/204DTD) and petrol (AJ200P/204PT) variants [EM-VERIFIED]. Quote data was captured at initial request, and final spend was confirmed via post-sale surveys conducted 3-6 months after replacement [EM-SURVEY].

Period: full calendar year 2025.

What this is not: this is not a claim about every F-Pace owner in the UK, only the pattern within our own customer base. Where a precise split isn't directly recorded in our systems, we've noted it as an editorial estimate rather than presenting it as an exact figure. Full detail on how we handle this distinction is on our data methodology page. As with all our case studies, no individual customer is named or identifiable in any figure or quote on this page.

The average actual spend was 12% below the initial quote, reflecting negotiation, choice of reconditioned over new, and some owners opting for used units. Diesel replacements were consistently cheaper, partly due to lower parts cost and wider availability of reconditioned AJ200D units [EM-QUOTE].

The Outcome

Satisfaction was high overall (86%), but varied by engine type and repair route. Diesel owners reported slightly higher satisfaction (89%) than petrol V6 owners (81%), likely due to the lower cost and simpler replacement process. Among those who chose reconditioned engines, satisfaction was 91%, compared to 78% for used units — the warranty and perceived reliability of reconditioned units clearly added peace of mind. The 8% who scrapped their vehicles all reported that the quote exceeded 60% of the vehicle's value, and they chose to cut their losses rather than invest further [EM-SURVEY].

The Pattern

"The gap between the initial quote and final spend was driven almost entirely by the choice of reconditioned over new — owners who shopped around saved an average of £800."

The 12% reduction from initial quote to final spend is a consistent pattern across our data, and it reflects the competitive nature of the UK engine replacement market. Owners who obtained multiple quotes (which our platform facilitates) typically achieved lower prices. The preference for reconditioned engines (72% of diesel replacements, 65% of petrol) also contributed to the lower final spend, as reconditioned units are cheaper than new or rebuilds while still offering strong warranties. Diesel owners were more likely to choose reconditioned due to the availability of high-quality units from the high volume of AJ200D engines in the UK fleet [PATTERN DATA].

Where the Minority Were Right

The 22% who chose used units weren't making a mistake — they were making a different trade-off. Used engines were typically chosen by owners with lower-value F-Paces (sub-£15,000) where the cost of a reconditioned unit would have pushed the repair-to-value ratio into the ⚠️ Watch zone. For these owners, a used engine kept the decision financially safe, even if warranty coverage was shorter. Similarly, the 8% who scrapped their vehicles were almost exclusively on examples over 8 years old with high mileage, where the repair cost exceeded 60% of the vehicle's value — the very scenario our Decision Framework warns against. These owners, while disappointed, made a financially rational choice.

What This Means For You

If you're facing an F-Pace engine replacement, the data suggests that diesel owners can expect quotes around £5,200, while petrol V6 owners should budget closer to £7,800. Shopping around and considering a reconditioned unit can save you around 12% off the initial quote. Always compare at least three quotes and use our Repair vs Replace Calculator to see if your vehicle value justifies the spend. For full engine-specific data, see the AJ200D Knowledge Centre or AJ200P Knowledge Centre, and the failure pages for common issues like timing chain or cooling system failures that often lead to replacement.

How This Compares to the Wider Picture

This 80-case F-Pace sample sits within a larger pattern visible across our full 2025 dataset covering 24,650 engine enquiries. The average quote for a diesel SUV engine replacement in our data is approximately £5,000, so the F-Pace diesel figure of £5,200 is slightly above average — plausibly reflecting the more complex Ingenium engine layout and higher labour requirements for the F-Pace's engine bay. Petrol V6 replacements (AJ200P and V6 variants) are less common overall (only 500 AJ200P requests across all Jaguar models in 2025), and the F-Pace V6 quote of £7,800 is in line with other premium SUVs. The scrap rate of 8% is similar to that seen on older XF and XJ models, reinforcing that vehicle value is the dominant factor in the scrap decision. As our dataset grows, we expect to refine these comparisons further.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost to replace an F-Pace engine?

In our 2025 sample, the average initial quote for a diesel (AJ200D) F-Pace engine replacement was £5,200, while the petrol V6 (AJ200P) averaged £7,800. Actual spend after negotiation was typically 12% lower.

Is it worth replacing an F-Pace engine?

It depends on the vehicle's value. Our data shows that if the replacement cost is under 60% of the vehicle's current market value, most owners are satisfied with their decision. Use our calculator to check your specific case.

What is the most common failure leading to F-Pace engine replacement?

In our data, timing chain failure on the diesel Ingenium and cooling system issues on the petrol V6 are the most common precursors to engine replacement. See our failure pages for detailed symptom and cost information.

Are reconditioned engines a good choice for the F-Pace?

Yes. Owners who chose reconditioned units reported 91% satisfaction, compared to 78% for used units, due to the warranty and quality assurance. Reconditioned engines also cost less than new, making them a cost-effective choice.
