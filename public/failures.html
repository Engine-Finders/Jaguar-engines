Emissions & Turbo

DPF Blockage

Immediate · £300–£1,800. The most common cause of restricted performance.

AdBlue / SCR Failure

Monitor · £350–£2,200. Often flags P20E8.

EGR Valve & Cooler Failure

Monitor · £400–£1,400. See P0401.

Turbocharger Failure

Immediate · £450–£2,400. See P0299.

448DT EGR & Swirl Flap

Monitor · Affects 448DT V8 diesels.

368DT Turbo & Injector Failure

Immediate · Affects 368DT TDV8.

Air Suspension

Compressor Failure

Catastrophic. The defining air suspension fault across the range.

Height Sensor Faults

Low. Often misdiagnosed as compressor failure.

Air Spring & Valve Block Leaks

Low. Common on higher-mileage L405 and L494.

Drivetrain

ZF 8-Speed Gearbox Faults

Monitor. Fitted across most modern Land Rovers.

Transfer Box & Differential Wear

Monitor. Aggravated by mismatched tyres.

Terrain Response System Faults

Low. Usually sensor or module related.

Electrical

Battery / Parasitic Drain

Low. Frequently triggers unrelated warning lights.

Infotainment & Software Glitches

Low. Often resolved by software update.

AJ20D6 Early-Build Issues

Monitor. Newer AJ20D6 - dataset still building.
