Powertrain & Timing Codes

Codes related to engine timing, camshaft position, and crankshaft synchronisation.

P0011

Camshaft Position 'A' – Timing Over-Advanced or System Performance (Bank 1)

P0016

Camshaft Position – Crankshaft Position Correlation (Bank 1 Sensor A)

P0011 (Bank 1) & P0016

Timing-related codes common on Jaguar V6 and V8 engines

P0300

P0303

Cylinder 3 Misfire Detected
