What the Results Mean

The oil grade (e.g. 0W‑20) is the viscosity recommended by Jaguar for your engine - using the wrong grade can affect fuel economy, cold‑start protection and engine wear. The capacity is the total amount including the oil filter; always check the dipstick after filling. The specification includes the ACEA and API standards and any Jaguar‑specific approval code (e.g. "Jaguar Land Rover STJLR.03.5003"). Using oil that meets these specs is essential for warranty and engine health. For detailed maintenance schedules, see our service schedule pages.

Where Our Data Comes From

Oil spec data is sourced from Jaguar technical publications, official service manuals, and cross‑checked with independent specialist feedback. Where we have verified UK service data, we note it as [THIRD‑PARTY]. Our editorial team updates the data whenever Jaguar releases a new bulletin.
