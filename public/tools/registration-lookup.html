What the Results Mean

The engine code is your vehicle's confirmed primary engine - for badges known to span two different engines across their production run (like certain D300 and D200 vehicles, covered in full on our AJ20D6 fitment guide), we'll flag this and point you to our engine number location guide to confirm. The verdict uses the same 60% repair-to-value framework published openly on our Decision Framework page - it's a starting guide based on typical values for your model, not a substitute for your own vehicle's specific valuation.

Where Our Data Comes From

Fault and cost data behind this tool is drawn from the same 2025 UK enquiry dataset behind every other page on this site - full detail on our Data Methodology page. Build and fitment data is cross-referenced against manufacturer records and independent parts suppliers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the registration lookup tool free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate is the engine identification?

Very high for the primary engine fitted to your model and generation. For badges known to span two engines across their production run, such as certain D300 and D200 badged vehicles, we show both possibilities and recommend confirming against your engine number.

What if my registration isn't recognised?

You can use our full Fitment Matrix to identify your engine manually by model, generation and build year, or check the engine number stamp directly using our Engine Number Location guide.
