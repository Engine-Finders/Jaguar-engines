What the Results Mean

The engine code is your vehicle's confirmed primary engine - for badges known to span two different engines across their production run (like certain D300 and D200 vehicles, covered in full on our AJ20D6 fitment guide), we'll flag this and point you to our engine number location guide to confirm. The verdict uses the same 60% repair‑to‑value framework published openly on our Decision Framework page - it's a starting guide based on typical values for your model, not a substitute for your own vehicle's specific valuation.

The year is derived from the 10th character of your VIN. The fuel type is determined by the engine code and model year. All data is drawn from our verified methodology.

If your VIN isn't recognised, you can use our full Fitment Matrix to identify your engine manually by model, generation and build year, or check the engine number stamp directly using our engine number location guide.

Where Our Data Comes From

Fault and cost data behind this tool is drawn from the same 2025 UK enquiry dataset behind every other page on this site - full detail on our Data Methodology page. Build and fitment data is cross‑referenced against manufacturer records and independent parts suppliers. Our VIN decoding logic is informed by official Jaguar VIN structure documentation and cross‑checked with real‑world examples from our specialist network.

We also draw on the Engine Failure Index 2026 and failure‑rate‑by‑engine reports to provide context for known faults.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the VIN decoder tool free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate is the VIN decoding?

Very high for the primary engine and model fitted to your vehicle. For models known to have different engines across their production run, we show both possibilities and recommend confirming against your engine number.

What if my VIN isn't recognised?

You can use our full Fitment Matrix to identify your engine manually by model, generation and build year, or check the engine number stamp directly using our Engine Number Location guide.

What do I need to know before buying a used Jaguar?

Use our VIN decoder to check the engine and model, then cross‑reference with our cost guides and owner decision data to understand potential repair costs.

Can I use this to check if my car has been recalled?

Our VIN decoder does not include recall data, but you can check our recall pages for specific models or use the official Jaguar recall checker.

How do I find my VIN?

Your VIN is typically located on the driver's side dashboard (visible through the windscreen), on the driver's door pillar, or in your vehicle registration documents. It is 17 characters long.
