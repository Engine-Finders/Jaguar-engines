What the Results Mean

The Total Annual Cost is the sum of depreciation, insurance, VED, servicing, MOT, fuel and average maintenance. This is the figure you should budget for each year. The Total Cost (3/5 years) shows the cumulative cost over those periods - a useful comparison if you're deciding between keeping your current Jaguar or buying a newer one.

All figures are estimates based on UK averages. Actual costs vary by region, driver profile and individual vehicle condition. For a repair‑specific decision, see our Decision Framework page.

Where Our Data Comes From

Vehicle values are sourced from AutoTrader, CarGurus and Motors.co.uk 2024–2025 [THIRD‑PARTY]. Insurance estimates use A‑BI insurance group data and UK average premiums [THIRD‑PARTY]. VED bands follow UK government rates for 2025/26 [THIRD‑PARTY]. Servicing and maintenance costs are based on Jaguar official service schedules and our own specialist interviews [EM‑INTERVIEW]. Fuel costs use UK average pump prices and official MPG figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Ownership Cost Calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate are the cost estimates?

We use the best available UK market data and Jaguar service schedules. However, individual costs vary by region, driving style and vehicle condition. Use the estimates as a guide, not a fixed quote.

Why is depreciation so high on some Jaguars?

Luxury vehicles, particularly the XJ and F‑TYPE, can depreciate faster than mainstream cars. Our estimates reflect real UK market trends over the last 12 months [THIRD‑PARTY].

What about electric Jaguars like the I‑PACE?

This calculator focuses on internal combustion engine models. For I‑PACE running costs, see our I‑PACE section.
