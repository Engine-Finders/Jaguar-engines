What the Results Mean

The diagnosis is a likely match based on the combination of symptoms, model and mileage you entered. It is not a substitute for a physical inspection - but it gives you a clear starting point and helps you understand whether the issue is Monitor, Immediate or Catastrophic. Each result links to a full Failure Page where you can read the root cause, diagnostic sequence and cost breakdown in detail.

All cost estimates use the same 2025 enquiry data and the 60% repair‑to‑value framework published on our Decision Framework page.

Where Our Data Comes From

Fault and cost data behind this tool is drawn from the same 2025 UK enquiry dataset behind every other page on this site - full detail on our Data Methodology page. Build and fitment data is cross‑referenced against manufacturer records and independent parts suppliers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Diagnostic Wizard free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How accurate is the diagnosis?

The wizard matches your symptoms against real failure patterns from our 2025 UK enquiry dataset. It is designed to narrow down the most likely causes, not replace a physical inspection.

What should I do after getting my result?

Each result links to a detailed Failure Page for that specific issue and a route to compare quotes from our vetted Jaguar specialist network. You can also run the numbers through our Repair vs Replace Calculator.

What if my symptom isn't listed?

We're adding more symptoms continuously. In the meantime, browse our Symptom Hub or use the registration lookup to identify your engine and its known faults.
