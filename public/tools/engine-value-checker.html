What the Results Mean

Supply‑only value is the typical price you'd pay (or receive) for the engine alone, based on current UK market rates from our proprietary data. Fitted cost adds independent labour at £65/hr (rates vary by region) - this gives you a realistic total for a replacement. Scrap/part‑out value is the estimated amount you could recover if the engine is beyond economical repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the engine value estimate?

Very high - based on real supply prices from 24,650 UK quotes in 2025. However, actual value can vary by region, demand, and specific vehicle fitment. Use it as a reliable starting point, and always get a specialist quote before selling or buying.

What does "supply‑only" mean?

Supply‑only is the price for the engine itself, without fitting. If you're selling, this is what a buyer might pay. If you're buying, you'll need to add labour and consumables.

Why does mileage affect value?

Higher mileage engines generally have more wear, reducing their market value. Our adjustment uses typical depreciation curves observed in UK classifieds and specialist feedback.

What if my engine is seized?

Seized engines have very low supply‑only value but may still have scrap/part‑out value (e.g., for cylinder heads, turbochargers, etc.). Our tool provides a separate scrap estimate to help you decide.

Can I sell my engine through this site?

We don't directly purchase engines, but we can connect you with our vetted specialist network who may buy or help you sell it. Use the compare quotes button to get started.
