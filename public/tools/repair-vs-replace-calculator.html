What the Results Mean

The Repair verdict means the typical replacement cost for your engine is comfortably within your vehicle's current market value - a repair is financially sensible. The Scrap Threshold verdict means the cost of a reconditioned engine approaches or exceeds 60% of the car's value. That doesn't mean you must scrap it - if the car has sentimental value, low mileage or is a collectable model, a specialist may still recommend repairing. The verdict is a starting point, not the final word.

The 60% threshold is the same framework we use across every page on this site, openly published on our Decision Framework page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Repair vs Replace calculator free?

Yes, completely free with no email required to see your result.

How does the calculator decide repair or replace?

It compares the typical replacement cost for your engine against your vehicle's current market value. If the repair exceeds 60% of the car's value, the calculator flags it as a scrap threshold case - the same framework we use across every page on this site.

My car is a classic - does the calculator still apply?

For genuinely collectable or low‑mileage examples, the financial threshold may not be the only factor. The calculator gives a market‑based verdict; we recommend also consulting a specialist who understands the classic market.

What if my model isn't listed?

We're adding more models continuously. In the meantime, you can use our engine value checker to get a rough estimate, or contact our team via the contact page for a manual assessment.
