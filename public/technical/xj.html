Technical Service Bulletins (TSBs)

Technical Service Bulletins are not safety recalls, but they address common issues that Jaguar dealers are advised to repair under warranty or as goodwill. The most frequently encountered TSBs for the XJ include:

  • TSB JTB00578 – AJ133 timing chain tensioner wear: updated tensioner and guide kit [PLACEHOLDER]
  • TSB JTB00456 – Water pump weep on AJ126 V6: revised pump and seal [PLACEHOLDER]
  • TSB JTB00399 – Supercharger coupler rattle on V6 and V8: replacement coupler and lubrication [PLACEHOLDER]
  • TSB JTB00277 – Battery drain due to infotainment module: software update [PLACEHOLDER]
  • TSB JTB00422 – Air suspension compressor wear on X351: revised compressor and relay [PLACEHOLDER]

If you experience any of these symptoms, ask your specialist or dealer whether the relevant TSB has been applied to your vehicle.

How to Check Your Own VIN for Outstanding Recalls

The most reliable way to check if your Jaguar XJ has any outstanding recalls is to use the official DVSA recall checker or Jaguar’s own VIN‑based portal. Follow these steps:

  1. Find your 17‑digit VIN – located on the driver’s side windscreen, door pillar, or in the service book
  2. Visit the DVSA website – gov.uk/check-vehicle-recall (external link)
  3. Enter your VIN – you’ll see any outstanding safety recalls immediately
  4. Alternatively, use Jaguar’s official recall portal – your local Jaguar dealer can also check for you

If a recall is outstanding, contact your nearest Jaguar dealer to arrange a free repair – safety recalls are always performed at no cost to the owner, regardless of vehicle age.

For more guidance on identifying your XJ’s specific engine and build details, see our engine number location guide.

What Isn't Covered by a Recall

It’s important to understand that recalls only address safety‑related defects that are common to a production batch. The following are not covered by a recall and will require paid repair:

  • Wear and tear items – brake pads, discs, clutch, tyres, suspension bushes
  • Serviceable components – oil, filters, spark plugs, belts
  • Failure due to lack of maintenance – e.g., sludge build‑up from skipped oil changes
  • Cosmetic issues – paint, trim, interior rattles
  • After‑market modifications – any non‑Jaguar parts may affect warranty coverage

If your XJ suffers a failure that is not covered by a recall, our failure pages and economics guides can help you decide whether to repair or replace.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check if my XJ has an outstanding recall?

Use the DVSA recall checker at gov.uk/check-vehicle-recall and enter your 17‑digit VIN. Alternatively, contact any Jaguar dealer.

What is the most common recall for the Jaguar XJ?

Based on available data, the AJ133 timing chain tensioner recall (pre‑2013) and water pump issues on AJ126 V6 are the most frequently reported. Always confirm with your VIN.

Can I check XJ recalls by VIN online?

Yes – the DVSA website and Jaguar’s official portal both offer free VIN‑based recall checks. No registration is required.

Are recall repairs really free?

Yes, safety recalls are always repaired free of charge by authorised Jaguar dealers, regardless of warranty status.

What should I do if my XJ has a recall but I’ve already fixed it myself?

You may be eligible for reimbursement from Jaguar, but you’ll need to provide receipts and proof of work. Contact your dealer for the specific procedure.

How often are recall lists updated?

We update this page regularly, but always check the official DVSA database for the latest information – it is the definitive source.
