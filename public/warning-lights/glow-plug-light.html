A flashing glow plug light on a Jaguar diesel - usually a yellow coil symbol - means the ECU has logged a fault that affects engine performance or emissions. It does not necessarily mean a glow plug has failed. The flashing light is the system's way of telling you to get the fault code read.

  • Most common causes: Glow plug failure, glow plug relay / module fault, injector issues, or a more serious engine fault such as timing chain wear [PATTERN DATA]
  • Often accompanied by the engine management light and stored codes such as P0670, P0671–P0674 (glow plug circuits), or P0200–P0263 (injector/cylinder faults)
  • Costs range from £50 for a single glow plug to £1,500+ for injector or timing chain work
  • Steady light at start‑up: normal preheat. Flashing while driving: stop and diagnose - a flashing glow plug light is a misfire or emissions-related warning

What the Glow Plug Light Actually Means

On a Jaguar diesel, the glow plug light - a yellow coil symbol - serves two completely different functions depending on when and how it appears.

Steady light at ignition on: This is normal. The glow plugs are preheating the combustion chambers to help the engine start in cold conditions. The light should go off within a few seconds (or up to 10–15 seconds in very cold weather). If it stays on for a long time or comes on while driving, that indicates a glow plug system fault.

Flashing while driving (or flashing at any time after start): This is not a preheat function. A flashing glow plug light on a Jaguar diesel is a warning that the ECU has detected a fault affecting engine performance, usually related to the fuel system, emissions, or timing. On Ingenium diesel engines in the XE, XF, and F‑Pace, the flashing glow plug light is often accompanied by a engine management light and may be the only warning you get before the car goes into restricted performance.

Crucially, a flashing glow plug light does not mean "replace the glow plugs." It means "read the fault codes." The light is a messenger - the stored codes tell you the message.

Steady vs Flashing - What Changes

The behaviour of the glow plug light tells you what level of urgency is required.

Steady light at start‑up: Normal preheat. If it stays on longer than usual, or comes on while driving, it indicates a glow plug system fault - likely a failed glow plug, relay, or wiring issue. This is not an emergency, but you should get it diagnosed before the next cold start.

Flashing light (any time after start): This is the critical state. A flashing glow plug light means the ECU has detected a fault that affects engine performance, emissions, or fuel delivery. It often indicates a misfire, injector fault, or in some cases, timing chain issues on Ingenium diesels. Do not ignore this - it's a warning that the engine is not running correctly and continued driving could cause further damage.

Flashing + EML: If the flashing glow plug light appears alongside the engine management light, the likelihood of a serious fault is higher. On Ingenium engines, this combination can indicate timing chain wear - a known catastrophic failure point on pre‑2019 units. See our Ingenium timing chain failure page for more.

What To Do In the Next 24 Hours

1

Read the fault code

The flashing light is not a diagnosis. Have the fault codes read by a specialist or a diagnostic tool. Most independents will do this cheaply or free if you're booking work.

2

Don't just replace glow plugs

A flashing glow plug light often has nothing to do with the glow plugs themselves. Replacing them without reading the code is expensive and rarely solves the problem.

3

Check for other warning lights

If the EML or restricted performance is also on, the fault is likely more serious - especially on Ingenium engines.

4

Listen for unusual noises

If you hear any rattling or knocking from the engine, do not drive. See our engine knocking noise guide for more.

Consider the engine type

Can You Drive With the Glow Plug Light On?

Steady light at start‑up (normal preheat): Yes - this is normal. If it stays on for a long time or comes on while driving, you can drive to a garage, but get it diagnosed soon.

Flashing light with no other warnings: Proceed with caution. The flashing light indicates a fault that affects engine performance. You can drive short distances at moderate speed, but get the code read as soon as possible. Do not drive for extended periods or under heavy load.

Flashing light with EML or restricted performance: Do not drive. This combination often indicates a serious fault - on Ingenium engines, it can be timing chain wear. Stop and recover the vehicle. See our restricted performance guide for more.

Accompanied by knocking or rattling: Do not start or drive. This indicates internal damage and requires recovery. See our engine knocking noise guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a flashing glow plug light mean on a Jaguar?

It means the ECU has detected a fault that affects engine performance or emissions - usually related to the fuel system, timing, or emissions components. It does not necessarily mean the glow plugs are faulty. The stored fault code is needed to identify the actual cause.

Can I reset the glow plug light myself?

If the light is due to a simple fault like a failed glow plug, replacing the faulty component and clearing the code may turn it off. However, if the light is flashing due to a more serious fault, clearing the code without fixing the issue will not prevent it from returning - and may destroy diagnostic data.

How much does it cost to fix a flashing glow plug light?

It depends on the cause. A single glow plug costs £50–£150. A glow plug module costs £150–£350. An injector can cost £400–£900. A timing chain on Ingenium can cost £3,500–£5,800.

Why did my glow plug light come on after a service?

Common causes: a sensor was not reconnected, the fuel system was disturbed, or the oil level is too high (a known Ingenium issue). A code read will tell you exactly why.

Is it safe to drive with a flashing glow plug light?

If the light is flashing and you have no other warnings, you can drive short distances with caution. If it's flashing alongside the EML or restricted performance, do not drive - recover the vehicle.

What does a flashing glow plug light on an Ingenium diesel mean?

On Ingenium diesels, a flashing glow plug light often indicates a fault with the fuel system, emissions, or timing. If accompanied by timing‑related codes (P0016, P0017), it can indicate timing chain wear - a known catastrophic failure point on pre‑2019 units.
