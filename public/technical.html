Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find the engine number on my Jaguar?
The engine number is typically stamped on the engine block. For most Jaguar engines, it's located on the lower front-left side of the block (near the oil filter housing) or on the rear of the block adjacent to the bellhousing. See the full guide with diagrams here.
Does the Ingenium diesel have a timing chain or belt?
The Ingenium diesel (204DTD) uses a timing chain, not a belt. However, early models (pre-2019) are known for tensioner wear. Full details on the 204DTD timing chain here.
What is the oil capacity of the AJ133 5.0 V8?
The AJ133 5.0 V8 has a dry fill capacity of approximately 7.8 litres (with filter). Always use oil meeting Jaguar specification WSS-M2C913-D or equivalent. Complete oil spec and capacity guide.
How often should I service my Jaguar XF?
The XF (X260) service interval is typically 12 months or 16,000 miles, whichever comes first. However, for high-mileage or heavily used vehicles, many specialists recommend a 10,000-mile / 12-month schedule. Full XF service schedule.
What does the "Restricted Performance" warning light mean?
The "Restricted Performance" light indicates the engine ECU has detected a fault and is limiting power to protect the engine. Common causes include fuel pressure issues, turbo problems, or DPF blockages. Full guide to Restricted Performance.
Can I use my VIN to check which engine is fitted?
Yes. The 8th character of your Jaguar VIN usually denotes the engine code. Our VIN Decoder tool can decode this for you, or you can use the Fitment Matrix to cross-reference.
