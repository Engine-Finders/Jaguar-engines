Frequently Asked Questions — Jaguar Data

Where does your data come from?

Our data is drawn from 24,650 real UK quote requests submitted via EnginesMarket.co.uk and EngineFinders.co.uk in 2025, combined with specialist interviews and post‑sale surveys. See our methodology page for full details.

How do I know your data is reliable?

Every number on our site carries a data tag — JAG‑VERIFIED, JAG‑QUOTE, PATTERN DATA, etc. — so you can see exactly what's proprietary, what's estimated, and what's third‑party. Our methodology is published in full.

Can I cite your data in my own research?

Yes. We encourage citing our data with proper attribution. Each data page includes a citation block with suggested wording and a permalink. See our editorial standards for citation guidelines.

How often is the data updated?

We refresh our primary data set annually. The Engine Failure Index 2026 is our flagship annual publication, but individual engine pages are updated as new patterns emerge.
