Step 1: Submit an Enquiry

Your journey starts with a simple enquiry through this site. You tell us what Jaguar you have, what engine code it carries (if you know it), and what symptoms or fault codes you're seeing. You don't need to have a diagnosis already — we'll help you narrow it down using our diagnostic wizard or by comparing your symptoms against our symptom hubs.

This enquiry is free and carries no obligation. We don't ask for payment details at this stage, and we never sell your data. Your enquiry is routed only to specialists who match your vehicle, engine, and location.

Step 2: Get Matched with Vetted Specialists

Once you submit your enquiry, we match you with specialists from our vetted network who are best placed to help. We consider:

  • Proximity to your location (where relevant)
  • Experience with your specific Jaguar engine family — we track which engines each specialist works on most frequently
  • Response time and availability — we prioritise specialists who reply quickly
  • Current workload — we avoid overloading any single workshop

You'll typically hear from one or two specialists within a working day. Every specialist in our network has been vetted against our published seven criteria, including written warranty provision, engine provenance, and complaint handling.

Step 3: Diagnosis and Quote

The specialist will discuss your vehicle's symptoms and may ask for additional information — diagnostic codes, service history, or recent work. We do not permit specialists to quote a full engine replacement without a proper diagnosis; that's one of our automatic disqualifiers.

Where a fault is confirmed, you'll receive a detailed quote that breaks down:

  • The unit cost (used, reconditioned, or rebuilt) — with provenance stated
  • Labour costs and estimated fitting time
  • Any additional parts or ancillaries required
  • The warranty period and what it covers — in writing

You are under no obligation to proceed at this point. We encourage you to use our repair vs replace calculator and our published 60% threshold to check whether replacement makes financial sense.

Step 4: Supply and Fitting

If you decide to proceed, the specialist will order the engine (or rebuild your existing unit) and schedule the fitting. The process typically takes between one and five working days, depending on engine availability and workshop workload.

Throughout the fitting, the specialist should keep you updated on progress. Our editorial standards require that we only work with specialists who have a documented complaint-handling process — so if something isn't going to plan, there's a clear channel to address it.

We do not get involved in the day‑to‑day fitting process, but we do track outcomes through verified owner surveys after the job is complete. This data feeds back into our vetting and helps us identify any specialist whose performance slips.

Step 5: Warranty and Aftercare

Every specialist in our network must provide a written warranty on both the engine unit and the labour. Our published warranty standards set out what we expect: a minimum period (typically 12 months), clear exclusions, and a straightforward claims process.

If something goes wrong within the warranty period, you contact the specialist directly. If the specialist is unwilling to honour a valid claim, we want to know — our verified outcomes process tracks post‑job satisfaction and flags patterns of poor behaviour. We don't mediate disputes, but we do use this data to inform ongoing vetting and routing decisions.

We also encourage you to leave feedback after the job — not as a public review, but as part of our internal quality monitoring. This helps us maintain high standards across the network and ensures that future owners benefit from the same level of care.

What Makes Our Process Different

Most 'find a specialist' sites stop at referral. We go further:

  • We publish our vetting criteria — seven specific checks, not a vague badge
  • We publish our warranty standards — so you know exactly what to demand
  • We publish our editorial standards — independence, data provenance, corrections
  • We publish our data methodology — how we source and tag every number
  • We have an expert panel — independent technical reviewers who verify factual accuracy
  • We tell you when to scrap — our repair vs replace framework includes the 60% threshold, and we use it honestly

We are not a garage, we are not a dealership, and we are not affiliated with Jaguar Land Rover Limited. We are an independent editorial resource that researches Jaguar engines, publishes what we find, and routes enquiries to specialists who meet our published standards. If you have any questions about the process, our contact page is the best place to start.
